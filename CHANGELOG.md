# Changelog

## 4.0.0
- New icon library: 387 symbols as toggle pairs (`-off` outline, `-on` filled) in all 15 colour themes, with and without background. Lights glow when on.
- New lights: spot light, built-in downlight, cupboard light, under-cabinet light, LED strip (straight and wave), key light (and Air), ring light, desk, floor, pendant, ceiling and wall lamps, lantern, candle, flashlight, stage light, floodlight, shelf light, neon sign, dimmer, light colour and more.
- Many more symbols from the common icon categories: arrows, shapes, files and folders, devices, camera and audio, layout and alignment, numbers 0 to 10, currencies, tools.
- Naming scheme documented in `docs/NAMING.md`; build tools in `tools/library`. Library PNGs are palette-optimised (54 MB for the whole pack).

## 3.1.0
- 16 new symbols: soundboard, mixer, equalizer, waveform, headset, broadcast, clapperboard, keyboard, mouse, grid, fullscreen, cast, metronome, French horn, thumbs up, send. Each in all 15 colour themes, with tile and without background. 4326 icons in total.
- New thumbnail, gallery, previews and animated preview.

## 3.0.0
- Every icon now also exists without background (`-clear`, transparent PNG). 3846 icons in total, one pack.

## 2.6.0

- 1923 icons in light, dark and burgundy variants for all four seasons plus standard.
- Added wrench and plus glyphs and 12 on/off icon pairs for Home Assistant keys.
- English names and tags.
