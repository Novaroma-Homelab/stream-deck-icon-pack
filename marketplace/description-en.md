Roman Season Icons: 27,546 icons for Stream Deck in five seasonal colour themes (Standard, Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter), each in light, dark and burgundy, with or without a tile background. Made for toggle keys: lights, media, devices and more.

What is inside
- 120 classic symbols for system, development, media, audio, streaming and study, including 12 on/off pairs for smart home keys (bulb, lamp, plug, TV, speaker, camera, Wi-Fi and more).
- 387 toggle symbols, each as an outline Off and a filled On version for two-state keys: lights that glow when on (spot light, downlight, cupboard and under-cabinet light, LED strip, key light, ring light, lamps), plus media, devices, files, arrows, shapes and numbers.
- 15 colour themes: 5 seasons x light, dark and burgundy. Every icon also exists without background (file names end in -clear) for use over a wallpaper.
- All icons are static PNG, 144 x 144 px, with English names and tags for the icon browser search. A blank key is included.

How to use
Install the pack, open the icon browser on a key, choose Roman Season Icons and search by name, for example "spot light". Use the matching Off and On icons for the two states of a toggle key. Light icons suit dark backgrounds and the other way round.

About
Companion to the Roman Season Themes plugin, fully usable on its own. Made with the help of an AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic) and reviewed by the author. Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
